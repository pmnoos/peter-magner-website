Stories, Memories & Life's Journey

Welcome to my little corner of the internet.

I'm Peter Magner, and this is where I bring together some of the things I've created, written and learned along the way.

Every life has a story worth preserving — and mine is no different.

Over the years I've worked, travelled, lived in different countries, raised a family and collected more memories than I could possibly fit into one lifetime.

It all started when I decided to write the story of my life for my family. That simple idea eventually led to other projects — preserving memories, recording everyday life and, now, reflecting on retirement and the journey of growing older.

There's still plenty of life left to write about.

WHERE IT ALL BEGAN

It All Started With My Story

It began with a simple wish — to leave the story of my life for my family.

I began writing down memories of my childhood, my years at sea, the places I had lived, the people I had known and the adventures along the way.

Slowly, those memories became My Story. That personal journey eventually inspired many of the ideas and projects you will find here.

ABOUT ME

A little about me

Everything here grew from one simple idea: that our memories, experiences and stories are worth keeping.

What began as a personal project has grown into different ways of preserving the past, capturing the present and enjoying the journey ahead.

I've worked, travelled, lived in different countries, raised a family and collected more memories than I could possibly fit into one lifetime.

Now, in retirement, I've discovered a new interest in writing, technology and creating projects that help preserve memories and share experiences.

I wrote, recorded and published my own autobiography, and I'd be glad to help you do the same. You're never too old to try something new!
