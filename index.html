WHERE IT ALL BEGAN

My Story

It all started with a simple wish—to leave the story of my life for my family.

I began writing down memories of my childhood, my years at sea, the places I had lived, the people I had known and the adventures along the way.

Slowly, those memories became My Story. That personal journey eventually inspired many of the ideas and projects you will find here.

ABOUT ME

A little about Peter

I'm Peter Magner, and I've been fortunate enough to live a life that has taken me on many journeys.

Along the way I have worked, travelled, lived in different countries, raised a family and collected more memories than I could possibly fit into one lifetime.

Now, in retirement, I have discovered a new interest in writing, technology and creating projects that help preserve memories and share experiences.

I'm certainly not a professional programmer or a technology expert—but I have learned that you're never too old to try something new!
